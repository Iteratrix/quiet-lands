# Elden Ring: Quest-Breaking Triggers Reference

A comprehensive list of actions that permanently lock out NPC questlines. Organized by
progression order. Critical for the Quiet Lands mod where the player can go anywhere at
any time without normal gates.

---

## THE THREE MAJOR POINTS OF NO RETURN (Base Game)

These three actions are the biggest quest-killers. Do everything possible before any of them.

### 1. Killing Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy
- **Ends ALL Volcano Manor questlines instantly** -- every NPC leaves the manor
- Locks: Tanith's assassination contracts, Patches' Manor letter, Bernahl's co-op invasion
- Lost rewards: Raging Wolf Set, Bull-Goat Set, Hoslow's Set/Petal Whip, Serpentbone Blade,
  Magma Whip Candlestick, Scaled Set, Taker's Cameo, Diallos' progression to Jarburg
- Rya/Zorayas' quest becomes impossible if you haven't spoken to her in the dungeon room
- Patches disappears (his bell bearing appears at Mt. Gelmir bridge)

### 2. Burning the Erdtree (Forge of the Giants / Fire Giant)
- Committing to the flame at the Forge after defeating Fire Giant
- Locks Corhyn/Goldmask quest if the Law of Regression riddle hasn't been solved yet
  (lose Mending Rune of Perfect Order -- an entire ending)
- Hyetta will leave permanently if you rest at any grace after obtaining Frenzied Flame
- Melina is permanently lost if you took the Frenzied Flame before this point

### 3. Killing Maliketh, the Black Blade (Crumbling Farum Azula)
- Transforms Leyndell Royal Capital into Leyndell, Ashen Capital permanently
- **Single biggest quest-breaker in the game -- locks ~30+ items across multiple questlines**
- Nepheli Loux/Kenneth Haight/Gostoc throne room scene becomes impossible
  (lose: Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones, Stormhawk Axe happy ending)
- Roundtable Hold NPCs relocate or vanish
- Fia must have reached Deeproot Depths and you must have chosen "No, I want to be held"
  before this point, or Lichdragon Fortissax becomes unfightable
- Ensha invasion must have been triggered (obtain Haligtree Medallion half first)
- Dung Eater's gaol sequence must be complete

## NPC-BY-NPC BREAKDOWN

### Ranni the Witch
*A mysterious four-armed witch and demigod daughter of Rennala. First met at the Church of Elleh in Limgrave (where she introduces herself as "Renna"), and later found atop Ranni's Rise in the Three Sisters sub-region of Liurnia.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Give Ranni Seluvis's Amber Draught | Ends her questline (reversible with Celestial Dew at Church of Vows) |
| Give Ranni the Fingerslayer Blade before finishing Seluvis | Seluvis dies, permanently losing Magic Scorpion Charm and puppet purchases |
| Start Radahn Festival before meeting Blaidd in Siofra River | Ranni won't appear at her Rise until Radahn is dead; miss Blaidd dialogue + Somber Smithing Stone |

**Safe order**: Talk to Ranni -> Meet her three servants -> Find Blaidd in Siofra -> Learn about
Radahn from Sellen -> Complete ALL Seluvis interactions (get Magic Scorpion Charm via Amber
Starlight) -> THEN give Fingerslayer Blade to Ranni.

### Preceptor Seluvis
*An arrogant sorcerer in Ranni's service who deals in puppets and potions. First encountered as a spectral projection at Ranni's Rise, then found in the flesh at Seluvis's Rise, also in the Three Sisters.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Give Fingerslayer Blade to Ranni | Seluvis becomes a lifeless puppet immediately |
| Miss: Magic Scorpion Charm (requires Amber Starlight from Altus Plateau) | Permanently unobtainable |
| Miss: Puppet purchases from his secret basement | Gone forever |

### Sorceress Sellen
*A sorcery instructor found in the cellar of Waypoint Ruins in central-east Limgrave, behind a Mad Pumpkin Head boss. She sells sorceries and is passionate about the history of glintstone magic.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Side with Jerren at Raya Lucaria Grand Library | Lose Lusat Set, Shard Spiral sorcery |
| Side with Sellen at Raya Lucaria Grand Library | Lose Witch-Hunter Jerren's rewards |
| Return Fingerslayer Blade to Ranni (kills Sellen connection to Scorpion Charm) | See Seluvis above |

Note: The summon signs for the Sellen/Jerren choice appear at Raya Lucaria Grand Library.
Rennala does NOT need to be alive for this.

### Nepheli Loux / Kenneth Haight / Gostoc
*Nepheli is a fierce warrior woman first found inside Stormveil Castle before the Secluded Cell; she can be summoned to help fight Godrick. Kenneth Haight is a self-proclaimed lord standing atop ruins in northeast Limgrave, asking you to reclaim Fort Haight on his behalf. Gostoc is the shifty gatekeeper at Stormveil's main entrance who suggests you take a side path into the castle.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Give Nepheli Seluvis's Potion | Kills entire questline -- lose the throne room ending |
| Kill Maliketh before: giving Nepheli the Stormhawk King ashes + defeating Morgott + exhausting Kenneth's "looking for a ruler" dialogue | All three NPCs fail to reach the Stormveil throne room |
| Kill Gostoc | Lose his shop (Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone) |
| Miss: 2x Ancient Dragon Smithing Stones, twin Stormhawk Axes | Permanently gone |

### Iron Fist Alexander
*A giant living warrior jar on a pilgrimage to become stronger. First found stuck in the ground in northern Stormhill, west of the Saintsbridge grace in Limgrave.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Kill him at Stormhill (first location) | Entire questline lost |
| Don't exhaust dialogue at Radahn arena AND Mt. Gelmir | He won't appear at Crumbling Farum Azula for final duel |
| Leave him stuck in the ground at first location or Liurnia | He can't progress to later areas |
| Kill Radahn via stake-skip without triggering Festival properly, then find Alexander at first spot | He becomes void -- can't be found anywhere (lose Shard of Alexander, best talisman in game) |

**Safe order**: Free him at Stormhill -> (optional) Gael Tunnel -> Radahn Festival (exhaust
dialogue) -> defeat Radahn (exhaust dialogue) -> Mt. Gelmir lava (exhaust dialogue) ->
Fire Giant (summonable) -> Farum Azula (final duel for Shard of Alexander).

### Bloody Finger Hunter Yura / Shabriri
*Yura is a ronin-style samurai who hunts those corrupted by Bloody Fingers. First found beneath a stone overpass northeast of the Seaside Ruins in southern Limgrave, where he warns you about the dragon at Agheel Lake. Shabriri is a wild-eyed figure who later appears at the Mountaintops of the Giants wearing Yura's armor, preaching about the Frenzied Flame.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Reach Mountaintops of the Giants | Yura dies, Shabriri takes his body |
| Miss: Ash of War: Raptor of the Mists (requires using his red summon sign at Raya Lucaria Second Church before Mountaintops) | Permanently unobtainable |
| Nagakiba drops wherever Yura last was, so the weapon itself is not lost | -- |

### Brother Corhyn / Goldmask
*Corhyn is a robed incantation merchant first found at Roundtable Hold, devoted to the Golden Order's teachings. Goldmask is a silent, emaciated figure standing motionless on a bridge in northern Altus Plateau, staring at the Erdtree. Corhyn eventually joins Goldmask as his devoted interpreter.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Burn the Erdtree before solving the Law of Regression riddle at Leyndell | Corhyn vanishes, drops only his bell bearing |
| Miss: Mending Rune of Perfect Order (an entire ending) | Permanently lost |

**Must do**: Find them in Leyndell -> solve the riddle (cast Law of Regression at the
statue west of Erdtree Sanctuary) -> THEN burn the Erdtree.

### Fia
*A woman in a hooded black dress found in a small bedroom at Roundtable Hold. She calls herself a Deathbed Companion and offers to hold you, granting a consumable that boosts poise at the cost of a small amount of max health.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Choose "No" when asked to be held (wrong dialogue choice) | Locks out Lichdragon Fortissax fight entirely |
| Kill Maliketh before giving her the Cursemark of Death and entering Deathbed Dream | Lose Fortissax fight and Mending Rune of the Death-Prince (another ending) |
| Kill Fia | Obviously ends everything |

### Dung Eater
*A menacing, cursed Tarnished in omen-horned armor who haunts a corpse-filled room at Roundtable Hold. He speaks only of defilement and loathing. His physical body is imprisoned in the sewers beneath Leyndell.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Give him Seluvis's Potion | He becomes a puppet; can't give him more Seedbed Curses |
| Lose access to Big Boggart's Seedbed Curse (Boggart killed by Dung Eater invasion before moving to moat) | One fewer Seedbed Curse available (need 5 of 6 total) |
| Miss: Mending Rune of the Fell Curse (yet another ending), Omen Set | Permanently lost |

### Rya / Zorayas (Volcano Manor)
*A polite young scout for Volcano Manor, first met in Liurnia of the Lakes east of the Scenic Isle grace. She asks you to recover her stolen necklace and, once grateful, offers an invitation to the manor. Her real name is Zorayas.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Kill Rykard before speaking to Rya in her dungeon room (after giving Serpent's Amnion) | She vanishes, quest impossible |
| Miss: Quest resolution (3 possible outcomes), Daedicar's Woe talisman | Gone |

### Diallos
*A well-spoken noble in ornate armor, first encountered at Roundtable Hold searching for his missing servant Lanya. He later appears in Liurnia near Academy Gate Town, grief-stricken and seeking revenge on recusants.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Kill Rykard before Diallos arrives at Volcano Manor | He disappears; Jarburg questline inaccessible |
| Never talk to Alexander | Jar-Bairn's dialogue in Jarburg won't progress (blocks Diallos finale) |
| Miss: Diallos's Mask, Numen's Rune, Jar-Bairn rewards | Gone |

### Patches
*A recurring trickster across FromSoftware games. In Elden Ring he ambushes you as a boss in Murkwater Cave (Limgrave) after you open his chest, then surrenders mid-fight and begs for mercy. If spared, he becomes a merchant.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Kill him in Murkwater Cave boss fight (don't stop attacking when he surrenders) | Entire questline dead; lose all gestures (Grovel for Mercy, Calm Down, Extreme Repentance, Patches' Crouch) |
| Kill Rykard without completing his Manor letter | He dies at Mt. Gelmir; lose Dancer's Castanets step |

### White Mask Varre
*The very first NPC you meet in the game, standing by The First Step grace in Limgrave. He wears a white surgical mask and cheerfully informs you that you are "maidenless." He later appears at the Rose Church in Liurnia.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Kill him | Questline dead (Mohgwyn Palace still accessible via Consecrated Snowfield teleporter) |
| Note: Quest requires 3 invasions (PvP or NPC Magnus at Writheblood Ruins offline) | Not a progression lock, just a requirement |

### Boc the Seamster
*A timid demi-human with a talent for tailoring, first encountered disguised as a small tree along the road south of the Agheel Lake North grace in Limgrave. Hit the tree to break the spell and free him. He offers to alter your armor throughout the game.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Give him a Larval Tear | He goes to Rennala, rebirth fails, he dies |
| Don't use "You're Beautiful" Prattling Pate (found at Hermit Village, Mt. Gelmir) before he asks for the Tear | He may die at Raya Lucaria |
| **Safe**: Get the Prattling Pate first, use it in front of him -> he stays alive as your seamster | -- |

### Hyetta
*A blind young woman who wanders Liurnia searching for a "distant light" she can sense through Shabriri Grapes. First appears near the Lake-Facing Cliffs grace in Liurnia after you have spoken to Irina on the Weeping Peninsula.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Kill Irina | Hyetta never spawns at all |
| Kill Edgar before he invades at Revenger's Shack (or miss his Shabriri Grape) | Can't complete quest (need all 3 Shabriri Grapes + 1 Fingerprint Grape) |
| Accept Frenzied Flame then rest at a grace | Hyetta leaves permanently |
| Note: Completing her quest LOCKS YOU INTO Frenzied Flame ending (reversible only with Miquella's Needle at Placidusax arena) | -- |

### Irina / Edgar
*Irina is a blind girl sitting by the roadside just across the Bridge of Sacrifice at the entrance to the Weeping Peninsula. She asks you to deliver a letter to her father, Edgar, who is the warden of Castle Morne at the peninsula's southern tip.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Kill Irina | Edgar never moves, Hyetta never spawns |
| Grab Revenger's Shack grace before Edgar arrives at Irina's body | Shabriri Grape may become unobtainable |

### Millicent / Gowry
*Millicent is a one-armed young woman afflicted by scarlet rot, first found at the Church of the Plague in Caelid. Gowry is an old sage in a rickety shack on the road into Sellia, Town of Sorcery, who asks you to find an Unalloyed Gold Needle to cure her.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Kill Millicent at any point | Cascading failure through Gowry's quest |
| Choose the red summon sign at end (challenge her) | She dies; different reward path |
| Must exhaust dialogue at each location and give Valkyrie's Prosthesis (Shaded Castle) | Or she won't appear at Prayer Room in Haligtree |
| Miss: Miquella's Needle (critical for reversing Frenzied Flame), Rotten Winged Sword Insignia | Big losses |

### Sorcerer Rogier
*A friendly sorcerer in a wide-brimmed hat, first found by the chapel altar inside Stormveil Castle. He can also be summoned to help fight Margit. After Godrick's defeat, he relocates to a balcony in Roundtable Hold.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Visit Ranni's Rise before Rogier tells you to | He enters permanent sleep early; lose some dialogue/direction |
| Not a major item loss -- his gear drops on his body regardless | Low stakes |

### Latenna the Albinauric
*An Albinauric archer found sitting beside her dying wolf companion at the Slumbering Wolf's Shack in Liurnia, accessible only through the Lakeside Crystal Cave. She can become a spirit ash summon if you earn her trust by showing the Haligtree Medallion (Right).*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Obtain Haligtree Secret Medallion (Left) from Castle Sol before meeting Latenna at Slumbering Wolf's Shack | She's already dead when you arrive; get her spirit ashes but can't complete quest |
| Miss: Somber Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone (reward at Apostate Derelict) | Permanently lost |

### Knight Bernahl
*A seasoned Tarnished knight in heavy armor who sells Ashes of War. First found at the Warmaster's Shack in Stormhill, Limgrave. He later joins Volcano Manor as "Recusant Bernahl."*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Kill him at Warmaster's Shack | Lose later invasion encounter and rewards |
| Don't exhaust dialogue at Volcano Manor | He never invades at Farum Azula |
| Kill Rykard before completing co-op invasion letter | Skip that step (but Farum Azula invasion still works post-patch) |

### D, Hunter of the Dead / D's Twin Brother
*D is a gold-armored holy warrior who hunts "those who live in death." First found in Stormhill, east of Stormhill Shack, mourning a corpse and warning you away from Summonwater Village. His silent twin brother can be found much later in a separate location.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Give D the Weathered Dagger -> he dies at Roundtable Hold, killed by Fia | This is the intended path |
| Give D's Twinned Set to his brother then reload | Brother becomes hostile -- be ready |

### Gatekeeper Gostoc
*The hunched, suspicious gatekeeper at Stormveil Castle's main entrance who advises you to sneak in through a side path. He secretly steals a percentage of your runes each time you die in the castle.*

| Trigger | What Breaks |
|---------|------------|
| Kill him (tempting because he steals runes) | Lose Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone from his shop after throne room scene |

## SHADOW OF THE ERDTREE DLC

### THE DLC POINT OF NO RETURN: Burning the Sealing Tree

After defeating Romina at Church of the Bud, you can burn the Sealing Tree.
**This removes ALL Miquella-aligned NPCs and fails their questlines.**

**Must complete BEFORE burning**:
- Leda's questline (all interactions up to Enir-Ilim)
- Sir Ansbach (give Secret Rite Scroll, get Letter for Freyja, but talk to Freyja FIRST)
- Thiollier (drink St. Trina's nectar twice after his invasion, or his summon signs won't appear)
- Hornsent (all interactions)
- Freyja (all interactions)
- Moore (all interactions)
- Dryleaf Dane (use "May the Best Win" gesture to fight him for Dryleaf Arts)

**Safe AFTER burning** (these quests survive):
- Queelign (Queelign's Greatsword or Queelign's Ashes)
- Count Ymir / Jolan (Cathedral of Manus Metyr questline)
- Igon / Fire Knight Priestess (Bayle the Dread questline)

### DLC-Specific Trigger: Defeating Messmer
- After killing Messmer in Shadow Keep, some NPC positions and dialogues shift
- Ansbach and Leda interactions change
- Not a full lock but advances quest states

### DLC NPC Summon Requirements
- **Ansbach**: Must give him Secret Rite Scroll or he can't be summoned at Enir-Ilim
- **Thiollier**: Must drink St. Trina's nectar twice or his summon signs won't appear
- **Dane**: Must challenge him ("May the Best Win") or he can't be summoned for Romina

| Defeat Morgott | Unlocks Mountaintops via Rold Medallion; Yura's clock starts ticking |

| Burn the Erdtree | Corhyn/Goldmask lock; Leyndell transformation queued |
| Kill Maliketh | Leyndell becomes Ashen Capital; massive NPC cascade |

## QUICK REFERENCE: SAFE PROGRESSION ORDER

1. **Limgrave/Weeping Peninsula**: Talk to every NPC. Don't kill Patches. Help Irina before
   doing anything else on the peninsula. Free Alexander. Meet Blaidd at Mistwood.
2. **Liurnia**: Complete Yura's encounters. Meet Ranni, start her quest. Do Seluvis's full
   quest before giving Ranni the Fingerslayer Blade. Get Latenna BEFORE Castle Sol medallion half.
3. **Altus Plateau / Mt. Gelmir**: Get "You're Beautiful" Prattling Pate for Boc. Visit
   Alexander at lava. Get Amber Starlight for Seluvis. Do Volcano Manor contracts.
4. **Volcano Manor**: Complete ALL assassination letters. Talk to Rya in dungeon. Finish
   Patches' letter. THEN kill Rykard.
5. **Leyndell**: Solve Goldmask riddle (Law of Regression). Progress Fia. Start Dung Eater.
6. **Mountaintops**: Get Rivers of Blood from Okina IMMEDIATELY. Yura dies here regardless.
7. **Before burning Erdtree**: Verify Goldmask riddle solved. Verify Fia is in Deeproot Depths.
8. **Crumbling Farum Azula**: Before killing Maliketh, verify: Nepheli has Stormhawk King,
   Kenneth quest done, Morgott defeated, Fia's Deathbed Dream entered.
9. **DLC**: Do ALL NPC quests before burning the Sealing Tree after Romina.
